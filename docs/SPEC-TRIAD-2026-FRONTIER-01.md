To prevent third-party entities, hostile forces, or alien environments from capturing, dissecting, or reverse-engineering Arcstone mechatronic assets, every node implements the **Clean Erasure Protocol**.


1. **Token B Instant Revocation:** The central or local gateway revokes the node's **Token B Ed25519 Key**, isolating it from the network and preventing it from being used as a rogue ingress bridge.
2. **Sub-5ms Memory Erasure:** Upon detecting physical hull breach or unauthorized casing disassembly, an internal circuit breaker triggers a sub-5ms FRAM/SRAM erase sequence. Key pairs, UEDO schemas, coordinate systems, and software models are completely vaporized.

Battery banks dump residual energy into internal high-resistance thermal sinks, safely fusing actuator joints and main logic boards into an inert, non-functional block of solid metal alloy.
